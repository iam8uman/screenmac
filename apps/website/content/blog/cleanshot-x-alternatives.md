The best CleanShot X alternative depends on why you are leaving. For a **free** screenshot tool with scrolling capture and OCR, try **Shottr**. For **Mac and Windows** with video and templates, **Snagit**. For **polished social images**, **Xnapper**. For a **free, local-only** capture app with annotation, redaction and recording, **Screenmac**. And if you only need the basics, the built-in **Shift-Command-5** tool may be enough.

None of them matches CleanShot X feature for feature, and for many people CleanShot X is still the right pick.

## Why do people look for a CleanShot X alternative?

CleanShot X is one of the most complete capture apps on the Mac. Its feature list covers area, window, fullscreen and scrolling capture, an annotation editor with blur and pixelate, recording to MP4 or GIF with microphone and computer audio, click and keystroke overlays, a video editor, OCR, pinned screenshots and its own cloud with share links. People who move away usually do so for one of these reasons:

- **Cost.** CleanShot's pricing page lists Basic at **$35 one-time** with a year of updates and 1 GB of cloud storage, with updates after that at $19 per year. Pro is **$10 per user per month**, billed annually.
- **Cloud they do not need.** Upload and link sharing are a big part of the product. Some teams cannot send captures to a third-party cloud at all.
- **More app than they use.** If you take a screenshot, add an arrow and paste it, much of the feature set goes unused.
- **Windows.** CleanShot X is Mac only.

## Shottr: the closest free alternative for screenshots

Shottr's site says you can "name your price, or enter $0" and that most features are available to free users. It covers scrolling capture in any app, OCR to the clipboard, a pixel ruler and a color picker, and is optimized for Apple silicon. The free version asks you to consider buying it every once in a while, and uploading needs an activated license.

**Pick it if** you want CleanShot-style screenshot tools without paying. **Skip it if** you need screen recording, which its site does not list. Our [Shottr vs CleanShot X](/blog/shottr-vs-cleanshot-x) comparison goes deeper.

## Snagit: for teams on Mac and Windows

TechSmith lists Snagit Individual at **$39 per user per year**. It captures scrolling pages, records the screen with microphone, system audio and webcam, turns clicks into step-by-step guides with Step Capture, and can blur emails, phone numbers and card details with Smart Redact. Videos can be hosted on TechSmith's Screencast.

**Pick it if** your team uses both Windows and Mac or writes a lot of documentation. **Skip it if** you dislike subscriptions. See our [Snagit alternatives for Mac](/blog/snagit-alternative-mac) for the reverse view.

## Xnapper: for screenshots that go on social media

Xnapper focuses on making a single screenshot look good: automatic balance and background color, social media size presets, one-click redaction of emails, card numbers, IP addresses and API keys, and OCR. Its pricing page lists one-time plans from **$29.99** for one Mac, with a year of updates. It is free to use with a watermark.

**Pick it if** you post screenshots to X, LinkedIn or a blog. **Skip it if** you need recording.

## Screenmac: free, native and nothing uploaded

Screenmac is the app this site is about, so weigh that. It is a native SwiftUI and ScreenCaptureKit app for macOS 14 Sonoma or later. It captures an area, a window or a display with its own shortcuts, opens an editor with arrows, shapes, text, numbered steps, highlighter, crop and **redact, blur and pixelate that are flattened into the pixels**, records the screen to MP4 with an optional microphone, and keeps a local capture history. There is no account and no upload feature.

What it does **not** have: scrolling capture, OCR, cloud links or a video editor. It is a free beta; builds are not yet notarized by Apple, and a paid Pro tier is planned but not for sale. **Pick it if** you want the capture-annotate-redact loop of CleanShot X without the cloud and without paying. You can try the editor first in your browser at [/editor](/editor), then [download Screenmac](/download).
