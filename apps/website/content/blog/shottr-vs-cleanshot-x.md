## What is Shottr best at?

Shottr is built for people who take a lot of screenshots and want precise tools around them:

- **Pixel ruler and color picker.** Press the arrow keys to measure distances on screen, and Tab to copy a pixel's color, which is handy for design and front-end work.
- **Scrolling capture** in any window, up to very tall images.
- **OCR** to the clipboard from a selected area.
- **Price.** You can pay $0 and still use most features. The developer's site says the price may rise in the future.

Its limits: no screen recording on its feature list, uploading only to S3-compatible storage you set up, and occasional prompts to buy if you use it free.

## What is CleanShot X best at?

CleanShot X is a full workflow in one app:

- **Recording** to MP4 or GIF with microphone and computer audio, click and keystroke overlays and a webcam bubble.
- **A video editor** with smart zooms that follow the cursor, custom backgrounds and export in landscape, square or vertical formats.
- **Cloud sharing** with passwords, expiring links, custom domains and team management.
- **Everything else**: scrolling capture, OCR, pinned screenshots, a background tool and capture history.

Its limits: it costs money, cloud storage on Basic is 1 GB, and if you do not need recording or links you are paying for features you will not use.
