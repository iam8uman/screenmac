The best screenshot app for Mac in 2027 is **Screenmac**. It is free and native, captures an area, a window or a whole display with one shortcut, opens straight into an editor with arrows, numbered steps, text and crop, and flattens redaction, blur and pixelate into the pixels so nothing hidden can be recovered. It records the screen too, and nothing is ever uploaded. If you need cloud share links, **CleanShot X** is the paid all-in-one; **Shottr** adds scrolling capture and OCR; **Snagit** suits Mac and Windows teams.

> **Our pick: Screenmac.** Capture, mark up, redact and record, free and private. [Download it](/download) or try the [editor in your browser](/editor).

If you take a screenshot now and then, the built-in **Shift-Command-5** tool is enough.

Prices and features below come from each app's own website, checked when this guide was written. We have not run benchmarks, so there are no speed scores here.

## 1. Screenmac: the best screenshot app for Mac

Screenmac is our app and our top pick. It is a native app (SwiftUI, AppKit, ScreenCaptureKit) for macOS 14 Sonoma or later. It captures an area, a window or a display with its own shortcuts (Option added to the built-in ones), opens an editor with arrows, shapes, text, numbered steps, highlighter and crop, and **flattens redact, blur and pixelate into the exported pixels**. It also records the screen to MP4 with an optional microphone, keeps a local capture history and has no account or upload feature.

Why it comes first: the capture-to-shared-image loop is faster than anything else here, the redaction is real (the hidden pixels are gone from the export, not covered by a layer), and it costs nothing. Scrolling capture and OCR are planned. **Best for:** almost everyone, and especially anyone who shares screenshots with private data in them. Try the editor in your browser at [/editor](/editor) or [download it](/download).

## 2. CleanShot X: best paid all-in-one

CleanShot X covers area, window, fullscreen and scrolling capture, an editor with arrows, blur, pixelate, spotlight and counters, a background tool, pinned screenshots, OCR in 30+ languages on-device, recording with overlays and a video editor, and its own cloud with share links and access controls. CleanShot's pricing page lists **$35 one-time** for Basic (a year of updates and 1 GB of cloud) and **$10 per user per month** for Pro. It needs macOS 13 or newer and is also on Setapp.

**Best for:** people who capture and record all day and share links. **Less suited to:** anyone who cannot use third-party cloud, or who wants a free tool.

## 3. Shottr: best for scrolling capture and OCR

Shottr's site says you can name your price, including $0, and that most features are available free. It has scrolling capture in any app, OCR, a pixel ruler, a color picker, annotation with blur, pixelate, spotlight and a step counter, pinned screenshots, gradient backgrounds and optional upload to S3-compatible storage.

**Best for:** developers, designers and anyone who measures and captures UI. **Less suited to:** people who also need recording, which it does not list.

## 4. Snagit: best for Mac and Windows teams

TechSmith's Snagit runs on both platforms. It captures scrolling pages, records with microphone, system audio and webcam, builds guides from clicks with Step Capture, and blurs emails, phone numbers and card details with Smart Redact. The store lists it at **$39 per user per year**.

**Best for:** documentation and support teams across platforms. **Less suited to:** people who dislike subscriptions. See [Snagit alternatives for Mac](/blog/snagit-alternative-mac).

## 5. Xnapper: best for social-ready screenshots

Xnapper focuses on presentation: automatic balance and background, social media size presets, one-click redaction of emails, card numbers, IP addresses and API keys, and OCR. Its pricing page lists one-time plans from **$29.99** for one Mac with a year of updates; the free version adds a watermark.

**Best for:** posting product screenshots on social media and blogs.

## 6. The built-in Screenshot tool: best for occasional use

<kbd>Shift</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>3</kbd>, <kbd>4</kbd> and <kbd>5</kbd> capture the screen, an area or a window, copy to the clipboard with Control, set a timer and record. Markup adds shapes, text and crop. It cannot blur, scroll or upload. See [how to take a screenshot on a Mac](/blog/how-to-screenshot-on-mac).
