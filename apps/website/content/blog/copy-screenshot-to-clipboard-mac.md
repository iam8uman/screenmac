To copy a screenshot to the clipboard on a Mac, **hold Control** with the normal shortcut: **Control-Shift-Command-4** to drag over an area, or **Control-Shift-Command-3** for the whole screen. Nothing is saved to the desktop; paste the image with **Command-V** into a chat, email or document. To make copying the default for every screenshot, press Shift-Command-5, open **Options** and choose **Clipboard** under **Save to**.

## What are the clipboard screenshot shortcuts?

| Capture | Save a file | Copy to the clipboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whole screen | <kbd>Shift</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>3</kbd> | <kbd>Control</kbd>-<kbd>Shift</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>3</kbd> |
| Selected area | <kbd>Shift</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>4</kbd> | <kbd>Control</kbd>-<kbd>Shift</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>4</kbd> |
| A window | <kbd>Shift</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>4</kbd>, <kbd>Space</kbd>, click | <kbd>Control</kbd>-<kbd>Shift</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>4</kbd>, <kbd>Space</kbd>, click |
| Touch Bar | <kbd>Shift</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>6</kbd> | <kbd>Control</kbd>-<kbd>Shift</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>6</kbd> |

Apple's guide says holding Control with any of the screenshot shortcuts copies instead of saving. The free [shortcut finder](/tools/mac-screenshot-shortcuts) builds the exact combination for you.

Press Control together with the other keys at the start of the shortcut.

## How do I make the clipboard the default?

If nearly every screenshot you take is pasted somewhere, skip the extra key:

1. Press <kbd>Shift</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>5</kbd>.
2. Click **Options**.
3. Under **Save to**, choose **Clipboard**.
4. Press **Esc**.

Now <kbd>Shift</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>3</kbd> and <kbd>4</kbd> copy instead of saving. To get files again, choose **Desktop** or a folder under Save to. See [how to change where screenshots are saved](/blog/change-screenshot-location-mac) for the other options.

## Where can I paste a screenshot?

Anywhere that accepts images:

- **Chat apps** such as Messages, Slack and Teams.
- **Mail** and most webmail compose windows.
- **Documents** in Pages, Keynote, Word, Google Docs and Notes.
- **Bug trackers and wikis** that support pasted images.
- **Image editors**, including the free [browser screenshot editor](/editor), which accepts a pasted image with <kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>V</kbd> so you can blur or mark it up before sharing.

Finder does not turn a pasted image into a file. For that, see [how to save a copied screenshot as a file](/blog/save-clipboard-screenshot-as-file-mac).

## Why won't my screenshot paste?

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Paste does nothing | The field accepts text only. Try another app or field |
| Pasted the wrong thing | You copied something else after the screenshot. The clipboard holds one item |
| No screenshot was taken | The shortcut may be taken by another app. See [Mac screenshot not working](/blog/mac-screenshot-not-working) |
| Image is huge | Retina screenshots are high resolution. Capture a smaller area |

The clipboard holds only the most recent item, so paste the screenshot before you copy anything else.

## Should I copy or save?

Copy when the image is going straight into one conversation or document and you will not need it again. Save when you might reuse it, need to attach it later, or want a record. You can also do both: take the screenshot normally, then drag the floating thumbnail into the chat or document before it disappears. The file still saves.

## Can I paste a copied screenshot on my iPhone or iPad?

Apple's Continuity features include Universal Clipboard, which lets you copy on one Apple device and paste on another nearby device signed in to the same Apple Account. Copy the screenshot with <kbd>Control</kbd>-<kbd>Shift</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>4</kbd>, then paste it on the other device shortly after. Check Apple's Continuity requirements page if it does not appear, or save the screenshot as a file and send it with AirDrop instead.

## How do I copy, then hide private details?

Pasting a screenshot straight into a chat means anything on screen goes with it. If the capture shows names, emails or tokens, paste it into an editor first:

1. Copy the screenshot with <kbd>Control</kbd>-<kbd>Shift</kbd>-<kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>4</kbd>.
2. Open the [screenshot editor](/editor) and press <kbd>Command</kbd>-<kbd>V</kbd>.
3. Redact, blur or pixelate the private parts.
4. Copy the result and paste it into your chat.

[Screenmac](/download) builds this into the capture itself: after each capture, its Quick Access preview lets you copy, save or drag the image, or open the editor first. Its shortcuts add Option, so the built-in Control shortcuts above keep working.
